# EA FIFA (10/11/12 era) — how AI-controlled teammates of a human player behave in ATTACK (off-ball support, runs, lanes, width/depth, tactics and instructions)

Scope note for the writer: FIFA 10, 11 and 12 (PS3/360, EA Canada) share one lineage of attacking off-ball AI that was introduced with FIFA 09's Custom Team Tactics (2008) and then rewritten for FIFA 13 ("Attacking Intelligence", 2012) and FIFA 14 ("Teammate Intelligence", 2013). EA has never published the algorithm; the best primary descriptions of the *mechanism* are (a) EA Canada's own 2002 soccer-AI patent (Voronoi-based positioning, runs, pass lanes), (b) EA's official FIFA 09 Team Management guide and manuals (the slider/formation-editor text that FIFA 10–12 reused verbatim), (c) the FIFA 12 gameplay-slider definitions, and (d) FIFA 13 developer interviews that describe, in detail, what the FIFA 12 run/support code did wrong. Everything later than FIFA 12 is labelled with its game year. Konami/Namco/Sega patents are included only where they document a mechanism the brief asks about (support placement, anti-clustering, space evaluation) and are clearly labelled as non-EA. Network note: several hosts (NeoGAF, Operation Sports full threads, Scribd, fifa-soccer.ru PDFs) were partly unreachable from this sandbox; where I could only read a search excerpt rather than the page, the finding is marked "(excerpt)".

## Key question 1 — What triggers a teammate run, how many runners at a time, and how is the run target chosen (space, lane to the carrier, behind the line, staying onside)?

### Takeaway
FIFA 10–12 teammates made runs when a space-ownership analysis said they could reach a piece of space (ideally beyond the offside line) before anyone else, when the human triggered one (L1/LB, or a one-two), and at frequencies set by Custom Tactics and (FIFA 12) a "Run frequency" slider; the run target was a point/end-point in space evaluated once, with straight-line locomotion, which is exactly what EA said it replaced in FIFA 13 (whole-run evaluation, curved runs, commitment, "two plays ahead"). No source states a hard cap on simultaneous runners.

### Cited Findings
- EA Canada's soccer-AI patent (inventors Jason Rupert and Geoffrey Harrower, filed 2002-10-22, granted 2009-09-15 as US 7,588,493 B2; application published as US 2003/0083117) divides the pitch into Voronoi cells with players as sites and uses the result "for determining where offensive and defensive players should position themselves on the field, determining when and where a player should make an offensive run, determining when and where a player should dribble the ball, determining when and where a player should pass the ball, determining tactical pattern matching, determining defensive marking logic, determining collision avoidance/detection" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en); [Justia US 2003/0083117](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20030083117)
- Run trigger and target in that patent: "Offensive runs are calculated using VD-AB [both teams, no goalies] ... If any player on the attacking team (Team A) has a Voronoi vertex on VD-AB that is closer to Team B's net than the offside line, then that player makes an offensive through run to that vertex. This models a player's behavior when the player realizes that he/she can get to that piece of space before anyone else on the field. Since this point is beyond the offside line, a run could result in a breakaway." — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- Offside is handled as a post-filter on candidate actions in the patent: "if an analysis determines that a potential receiver is in fact out-of-bounds or offsides, the analysis might be adjusted to not cause a pass to that person" (step S4, "constraints imposed by rule, such as the offsides and out-of-bounds rules") — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- The patent's Voronoi sites are forecast positions, not current ones: "a forecasted future position of the player might be used instead, where the forecast is based on the player's current position and current momentum ... By offsetting the player's position forward a bit, in the direction of motion, and in proportion to the player's speed, a closer approximation of the diagram is possible"; the six diagrams (each team with/without GK, both teams with/without GKs) are computed with Fortune's sweepline algorithm seeded from the previous frame's diagram, and "the work of calculating the diagrams can be split up over three frames" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- Human-triggered runs, FIFA 09 (same control in 10–12): "Instigate Forward Run — Press L1 to trigger a forward run by a nearby team-mate"; "One-Two Pass — Press L1 + (X) to initiate a one-two pass, press X again, before receiving the pass, to return the ball to the original possessor" — [FIFA 09 Basic Controls (EA guide PDF)](https://fifa-soccer.ru/fifa09guide/FIFA_09_Basic_Controls.pdf)

- Namco (not EA) GB 2365362 A: the user marks a target point and "the ally character standing nearest to the position 'A' to which the own character wants to pass the ball runs into the position 'A'"; variants restrict which ally may run into each pitch zone — [Google Patents GB2365362A](https://patents.google.com/patent/GB2365362A/en)

### Inferences
- The EA patent's rule ("any attacking player with a Voronoi vertex beyond the offside line runs to it") has no runner cap, so simultaneous runs depended on geometry; the FIFA 09 Crossing slider text ("how many players will look to get into the box") and the FIFA 12 Run-frequency slider ("number of runs teammates will make") are the only count controls, so the count was tuned by sliders, not by an explicit N-runner rule.
- McHardy's FIFA 13 account is the best evidence of FIFA 12's run execution: pick an end point, re-evaluate frequently (visible as 3–4 changes of mind per run), straight-line path, re-target to a worse point when blocked; onside correction by curving the line of the run. A sim reproducing FIFA 10–12 should therefore implement end-point selection plus commitment/hysteresis to avoid the FIFA 12 "zigzag" failure the next version fixed.
- Rutter's "players creating passing channels to allow you to play the ball to them" (FIFA 13) and the 2002 patent's "teammates move towards these [passing-lane] lines" show the same lane-seeking primitive persisted from the patent era into the FIFA 13 rewrite.

### Gaps
- No EA source states how many teammates may run at once in FIFA 10–12, or the re-evaluation interval of a run; the "3–4 changes of mind" remark is the only quantitative hint.
- No EA source gives the run end-point scoring formula (space value vs lane vs goal distance) for FIFA 10–12; the Voronoi-vertex rule is from a 2002 patent whose use in the shipped FIFA 10–12 code is not confirmed.
- NeoGAF's FIFA 12 thread (manual quotes) and the EA FIFA 12 "Rutter answers your questions" page could not be read (bot block / 404).

## Key question 2 — How are short support options placed (distance, angle to carrier, relative to nearest defender and passing lane)? Any numbers?

### Takeaway
EA documents support placement qualitatively: nearest teammates "come to support" under short passing, far players "find space" under long passing, and (per EA's 2002 patent) supporters move toward lines from the ball holder to his Voronoi neighbours and are passed to along the intersection of the carrier's facing vector with the receiver's Voronoi cell. The only hard numbers for support geometry come from non-EA patents (Konami pass-partner search cone/distance; Namco 0.5 s / 15 m / 6 s space thresholds).

### Cited Findings
- EA patent, lane-seeking support: "various Voronoi diagrams might be examined to identify a player closest to the ball and teammates and/or opponents that are the player 'nearest neighbors'. From lines drawn from player O to neighbors, passing lanes can be determined and then another Voronoi diagram might be used to calculate which teammates are closest to these passing lanes. Based on that information, teammates move towards these lines to position themselves for a pass" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- EA patent, pass target geometry: "the computer intersects BH's [ball holder's] facing vector and the Voronoi cell of receiver(s) to get a range of possible reception points along that line of intersection ... the set of points where receiver 613(a) would receive the pass and can reach before any other player ... the analysis could look for the points within a Voronoi cell of a receiver that leads the receiver the most, while still ensuring the receiver will get to the ball first. Taking into account the intersection line segments, a suggested power for a ball kick could be determined" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- EA patent, definition of "ownership" used for support and marking: "one or more of the Voronoi diagrams ... might be used any time the tactical engine needs to determine who 'owns' a point on the field ... Ownership of a point is useful for passing and marking purposes, as it indicates who can get to that point first"; weighted/non-linear metrics are proposed so that "players on a team can be assigned strength, agility, and speed attributes with Voronoi division being done accordingly" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- EA patent, pass-target example: "the target for the ball might be along a line segment 14 that divides the Voronoi cell 16 of player 11. Because of the Voronoi division, it is known that all of the points on line segment 14 are closer to player 11 than to any other player" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- EA's FIFA 09 Team Management guide (text reused for FIFA 10–12 Custom Tactics), Build-Up Passing: "Short – The team will try to build using short passes. Players closest to the ball will come to support ... Long – ... Players farther from the ball possessor will attempt to find space. Close players will anticipate the long ball and push up to look for the knock down or flick on"; "In a short passing system, you will see more 'check to' runs by players as they drop back into space to receive the ball. The team will arrange itself in such a way that there are multiple short passing options available" — [EA FIFA 09 Team Management guide PDF (excerpt)](https://fifa-soccer.ru/fifa09guide/TeamManagement.pdf); same text at [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Custom Tactics](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html)
- Same guide, Chance Creation Passing: "Safe – ... Players will ensure that the ball possessor has safe options when positioning themselves, and will wait for a good opportunity to make a penetrating run ... Risky – ... Players look for every opportunity to make runs into space and behind the defence. Teammates won't be as concerned about ensuring safe options for the ball possessor"; Shooting "Lots – ... Teammates will make the extra effort to position themselves looking for shooting opportunities, for example staying just outside the box looking for a layoff, or positioning themselves for the cutback" — [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Custom Tactics](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html)
- Same guide, how the human team differs: "For Human controlled players, the Custom Tactics settings influence the way the other nine field players move in relation to the player that you are controlling"; "Ultimately, you control when and where you pass, but the CPU will position your support players in good position to maintain possession" — [EA FIFA 09 Team Management guide PDF (excerpt)](https://fifa-soccer.ru/fifa09guide/TeamManagement.pdf); [TheGameReviews](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html)
- FIFA 12 attribute that governs support placement (EA Backpage): "Att. Position ... deals with a player's ability to spot open space and move into good positions that offer an attacking advantage. The higher this stat, the more likely a player is to make enough space to receive the ball in dangerous areas"; "Vision ... The higher the score in this attribute, the further the player will be able to see" — [EA Backpage FIFA 12 Attributes Guide](https://www.ea.com/news/fifa-12-attributes-guide-01)
- EA's FIFA 12 formations writer observing the support AI: in a 3-4-2-1 "the central midfielders will actively support your attacks ... especially effective if you hold the ball up during a quick break and allow one or both to join the play late"; "the ST often turns towards the play when you have the ball so that he becomes an option to pass to, but this can leave you with no central figure in the box" — [EA Backpage FIFA 12 Formations Guide Part 2](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/news/fifa-12-formations-guide-part-2-the-backpage)
- FIFA 13 (label) on separation from the marker: supporters "drift laterally to create a separation between them and the defender giving you an option to play the ball to feet" — [UltimateFIFA (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130826080611id_/http://ultimatefifa.com/2012/fifa-13-ai-improvements-attacking-intelligence/)
- Konami (not EA) WO 2008/026382: pass-partner search area = sector within reference angle θ2 of the stick direction and reference distance R2 from the carrier, set narrower than the player's visual-field area (θ1, R1) whose size "is determined based on the value of the visual field parameter of the operation target player object"; among teammates in the area the one with the smallest distance is selected first; the pass may be aimed at the teammate's predicted future position — [Google Patents WO2008026382A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2008026382A1/en)
- Konami (not EA) US 2011/0207532: "a player character suitable as a pass target refers to ... a player character having no player character of the opponent team positioned nearby (hereinafter referred to as a 'player character which is free')"; "an area suitable as a movement destination refers to an area having no player character positioned nearby (hereinafter referred to as an 'open space')"; candidates are "acquired ... based on the positions of the player characters belonging to the second team" — [Google Patents US20110207532A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20110207532A1/en)
- EA (hockey, 2018, label) US 10,839,215: rule-based NHL pass logic "determining the position of the puck carrier, determining a position of a teammate that is a pass recipient, calculating a path between the puck carrier and the pass recipient, calculating whether an opponent can reach the path to intercept the puck, and evaluating whether or not the pass recipient is in a better position to score" — [Google Patents US10839215B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US10839215B2/en)

### Inferences
- In EA's framing a "good support position" is a point the supporter owns (reachable first) that lies on or near a passing lane from the carrier and is forward-led by the receiver's velocity; distance to the carrier is not a tuned constant but falls out of the short/long passing slider (nearest players close in; far players spread).
- Because the pass target is taken along the carrier's *facing vector*, FIFA's support positions are implicitly placed relative to where the carrier faces, which matches the user-visible rule that L1 triggers the run of the teammate you are facing.

### Gaps
- No EA source gives support distances, angles, or minimum separation from the nearest defender in metres for FIFA 10–12. The only numeric thresholds found are in Namco's and Konami's patents, which document their own games.
- The EA FIFA 09 guide PDF could only be read through search excerpts (host blocked); the quoted passages were cross-checked against TheGameReviews' copy of the same text.

## Key question 3 — How do Attacking Work Rate and the "Stay forward"-style instructions change where a player is during build-up and defensive transitions?

### Takeaway
In FIFA 09–13 the per-player levers were the formation editor's Work Rate (attacking = how often the player considers forward runs; defensive = how much priority he gives to getting back) and Positioning (an attack-phase and a defence-phase positional offset, explicitly "not runs"), plus the team-wide D-pad mentality; the named FIFA 15+ instructions (Stay Forward, Get In Behind, Come Short, Cut Inside, Stay Wide, Free Roam, Target Man) did not exist in FIFA 10–12.

- FIFA 12 manual, identical editor: "decide which aspect of his game you want to alter (Player Base Position, Player Work Rate, or Player Positioning)"; "Press the directional buttons to change your team's mentality on-the-fly" — [FIFA 12 manual (ManualMachine)](https://manualmachine.com/electronicarts/eafifasoccer12/340817-user-manual); FIFA 13 manual, same three options — [FIFA 13 manual p.16 (ManualsOnline)](https://laundry.manualsonline.com/manuals/mfg/electronic_arts/14633197587.html?p=16)

### Inferences
- In the FIFA 10–12 model a player's phase position = formation base position + attack-phase offset (or defence-phase offset) chosen in the editor, with Attacking Work Rate acting as a probability/frequency gate on run consideration and Defensive Work Rate as a priority weight on recovery; the "Stay forward"-type instructions of later games are coarse presets over these same two quantities.
- FIFA 11's attribute work rates (Personality+) and the editor's manual Work Rate are the same lever exposed two ways, which is why the FIFA 12 editor still lists "Player Work Rate".

### Gaps
- No EA text quantifies the offset magnitude of "Positioning" adjustments or the frequency scale behind Low/Medium/High attacking work rate.
- EA's own FIFA 12 "4-1-5 anyone" and FIFA 13 "Using Custom Formations" Backpage articles (which discuss per-player work-rate settings) now return 404 and could only be seen as a search excerpt ("Low attacking setting to limit their forward runs, and a high defensive setting to keep them as cautious as possible") — [EA Backpage FIFA 12 4-1-5 (excerpt, page now 404)](https://ea.com/en-gb/news/the-backpage-fifa12-4-1-5-anyone).

## Key question 4 — How does the attacking AI avoid clustering (two players to the same space) and keep team width/depth?

### Takeaway
EA's documented anti-clustering is geometric: Voronoi ownership assigns each piece of space to one player (so two attackers cannot both "own" the same vertex), dribbling/running steers to the nearest Voronoi vertex ("avoiding teammates and opponents"), FIFA 10 added a collision-avoidance system and "Face Ball" logic for getting out of the way of passes/shots, and width/depth were set by Custom Tactics (Team Width, Positioning Organised/Free Form) and FIFA 12's Line Length/Line Width sliders; Konami's patent adds an explicit radius-based "move teammates out of the user's target area" rule.

### Cited Findings
- EA patent, collision-avoiding movement: "to have a player with the ball to dribble to a target point on the field, the player should dribble towards the Voronoi vertex from VD-AB that is closest to the target point. The resulting behavior causes the player to arrive at the point quickly, while avoiding teammates and opponents, moving in a manner that looks very much like a soccer player, including feints and abrupt heading changes"; the same analysis "can be used by the defense to identify holes in their defensive positioning, and adjust accordingly" — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- FIFA 10 EA blog: "Positioning: ... A new system call 'Position Priority' makes sure that Defensive Midfielders cover at the back if a Centre back has gone up for a corner – and they won't leave their post until it's safe"; "Urgency: ... When players aren't involved in immediate 'action' they're more relaxed. As play gets closer they become more alert and responsive"; "new Face Ball logic, to ensure that players are correctly following the play ... This work even moves into players getting out of the way of teammates passes and shots" — [TheSixthAxis, EA Talk FIFA 10](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2009/06/16/ea-talk-fifa-10/)
- FIFA 10 feature lists: "a new collision avoidance system" — [Gamepressure FIFA 10](https://www.gamepressure.com/games/fifa-10/z2765); "New collision avoidance system means that players are less likely to get in the way and collide with team mates" (PS2 build) — [GameSpy FIFA 10 PS2](http://ps2.gamespy.com/playstation-2/fifa-soccer-2010)
- Width/depth via Custom Tactics (FIFA 09 manual definitions, kept in FIFA 10–12): "Team Width – How far across the pitch a team shifts towards the ball. Defender Line – The shape and strategy (e.g., offside trap vs. defensive depth) of the defensive line"; Build Up / Chance Creation "Positioning – A team's freedom of movement within their selected formation" — [FIFA 09 Xbox 360 manual](https://videogamemanual.com/xbox360/FIFA%2009.pdf)
- Free Form vs Organised in attack (EA guide): "Organized – Players tend not to stray too far from their assigned positions ... Free Form – Players have the freedom to make runs out of position. For example, forwards can make more runs wide to collect the ball, fullbacks can make more overlapping runs, wide players can cut in more, and central midfielders can make runs to the corner or overlap the strikers"; "Team set to Free Form will send more Fullbacks on overlapping runs. You will see Wing Midfielders pull in centrally. Your Forwards will make more wide runs and the Central Mids will overlap the strikers with curved runs"; "Organized positioning will cause players to stay in their area of the field as opposed to running free. They will make a move to space, but their priority is to keep the team's shape" — [EA FIFA 09 Team Management guide PDF (excerpt)](https://fifa-soccer.ru/fifa09guide/TeamManagement.pdf); [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Custom Tactics](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html)
- FIFA 10 Custom Tactics guide: Build-Up Positioning "Determines the freedom of movement in the first two thirds of the pitch. When coupled with a team who has players with high positional stats, free-form is very effective. Organized allows for more concrete attacks" — [FIFA Encyclopedia FIFA 10 Custom Tactics](http://www.fifaencyclopedia.com/10customtactics.php)
- FIFA 12 shape sliders: "Positioning: Line height — Specifies how high/low the defensive line will position themselves. Positioning: Line Length — Specifies how stretched or compact the team will try to keep their line for the pitch. Positioning: Line width — Specifies how stretched or compact the team will try to keep for the width of the pitch" — [Neoseeker FIFA 12 slider list](https://www.neoseeker.com/forums/54562/t1676903-gameplay-slider-list/)
- Box occupancy for crosses is explicitly multi-player and timed (EA guide): Crossing "affects the timing of when players make runs into the box, and how many players will look to get into the box ... Lots – ... The ball possessor will try to cross the ball early. Teammates will make earlier runs into the box"; observed: "Notice that we have three players lined up in case we cross. The near post, back post, and middle of the box are all covered" — [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Custom Tactics](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html)
- FIFA 12 PPI set-piece/box placement by attribute: "Where a player sets up during a corner kick, how they run off the ball, and how they approach a player on defense, will all be determined by their individual strengths" — [GameRant FIFA 12 features](https://gamerant.com/fifa-12-new-features/); Rutter: "Crouch, he's going to go the back post because that's where he's effective ... If it's Defoe, maybe they will look to play short" — [VGChartz Rutter interview](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/87683/interview-with-fifa-12-producer-david-rutter/)
- Konami (not EA) US 2010/0240429 anti-cluster rule: when the user sets a movement target for his player, any teammate inside "a decision area ... lying within a predetermined distance (R) from a movement target position" is moved "to a position shifted by a predetermined distance (L) from the current position ... in a direction of a straight line extended from the movement target position ... toward the current position of the teammate"; "The predetermined distance (L) is set equal to or greater than ... the radius (R)"; the move is kept inside the pitch; the decision-area size can scale with the distance between the user's player and the target; and a "teamwork level parameter" can gate whether and how quickly (with a wait) the teammate yields — [Google Patents US20100240429A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20100240429A1/en)
- Konami (not EA) WO 2008/026382 chooses among teammates in the pass cone by nearest-first ordering, i.e. one receiver is selected per pass — [Google Patents WO2008026382A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/WO2008026382A1/en)
- Sega (not EA) US 7,300,345: deploys players to "optimum positions" from stored base positions and ability parameters for the chosen formation — [USPTO US7300345B2](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/7300345)

### Inferences
- EA's approach to "two players to the same space" is ownership-based rather than reservation-based: a Voronoi vertex belongs to exactly one site, so a second player steering to "his" nearest vertex is steering to a different point; the reader's reserve-a-destination rule is a discrete analogue.
- Width and depth in FIFA 10–12 were not emergent from support logic alone; they were team-level parameters (Team Width, line sliders, Organised/Free Form) that clamp how far a player may stray from his formation slot in each pitch third, with Free Form specifically enabling cross-lane movement (wide players inside, mids overlapping strikers).

### Gaps
- No EA source describes a runtime "reservation" of run destinations or a minimum inter-teammate spacing in metres.
- The FIFA 10 PC build's 26 "Team Styles" (Wingplay, Target Man, Counter Attack, Attacking Full backs, Total Football, The Hole, Box Overload, Side Overload, 3rd Man Release; defensive Offside Trap, Pressing, Zonal Marking, Flat Back, Clearance, Man Marking) are listed but never explained; the promised EA follow-up blog was not found — [FIFA-Infinity, FIFA 10 PC Gameplay Innovations Blog](https://www.fifa-infinity.com/fifa-10/fifa-10-gameplay-innovations-blog). Note the FIFA 10 PC game was a different engine from the PS3/360 game.

### Inferences
- The year-over-year pattern is: FIFA 09 introduced the tactics-driven support model; FIFA 10 added urgency/position-priority and curved onside runs; FIFA 11–12 layered attribute personality (work rates, vision, traits) on top without changing the run/support core; FIFA 13 rewrote that core. ## Additional required topic B — FIFA 11 Personality+ and FIFA 12 Pro Player Intelligence as they affect off-ball attacking play; attribute roles

### Takeaway
Personality+ (FIFA 11) made attributes/traits drive execution "on and off the ball" and split work rate into attacking/defensive; Pro Player Intelligence (FIFA 12) made players and their teammates aware of each other's strengths, so run targets (e.g., an "aerial threat" going to the back post, a poacher coming short) and how quickly a passer picks out a distant runner (Vision) depended on who was on the pitch; the attributes EA names for off-ball attack are Attacking Position (spot space, move into dangerous areas), Vision (how far the passer sees), and the work rates.

### Cited Findings
- Personality+ (Rutter): "The gameplay is now fully driven by player attributes and traits— both on and off the ball. - Personality+ in-game traits (Spanish guide): for players rated 75+ you can assign special abilities ("habilidades especiales") — [Meristation FIFA 11 guide](https://as.com/meristation/2010/11/25/guia_pagina/1290668414_033599.html)

### Inferences
- PPI is best modelled as (a) per-player run-target preferences keyed by traits (aerial threat → far post; poacher → come short; long shooter → edge of box for cutbacks) and (b) a passer-side perception radius/latency scaled by Vision that determines how far away a runner can be and how soon he is "seen".

### Gaps
- EA's official Personality+ press release ("FIFA 11 Redefines Player Authenticity") and the FIFA 12 "Rutter answers your questions" page are offline (404); the "36 attributes / 57 traits" figure appears only in retailer copies of marketing text and is not independently sourced here.

## Additional required topic C — Differences between a human-controlled team's AI teammates and a fully CPU-controlled team

### Takeaway
EA's documentation draws the line explicitly: for the CPU team the tactics settings drive both positioning and the pass/shot/run decisions, whereas for the human's team they only drive how the other players move relative to the controlled player (the human still decides when/where to pass and shoot); FIFA 12's PPI press copy is phrased as "CPU players" and FIFA 12's sliders are set separately for user and CPU, while community reports describe CPU players chasing loose balls and making runs more readily than the user's teammates.

### Cited Findings
- EA FIFA 09 guide: "There are two components to the Custom Tactics. The CPU both positions players and executes based on the team's Custom Tactics settings. For Human controlled players, the Custom Tactics settings influence the way the other nine field players move in relation to the player that you are controlling"; "For the CPU, passing will affect both the types of runs and the choices that are made for passes in the attacking third. For your team, the passing setting will determine how your players position themselves. A Safe team concept will provide you with more support"; "CPU teams with Shooting set on Lots will blast away at the goal ... - Structurally, FIFA 10–12 ran one off-ball positioning model for both sides, but on the human's side the "decide to pass/shoot/dribble" layer is replaced by the human, so the human's teammates can only *offer* options (support, runs) and must be prompted (L1, one-two) for runs the CPU side would generate itself; the louder criticism of the human's teammates ("stand around") is consistent with reaction thresholds tuned to the ball carrier's decisions, which a CPU carrier makes continuously and a human does not.

### Gaps

## Additional required topic D — What FIFA 10–12 does that a deterministic 60 Hz sim with formation slots, phases, presser+cover, marking, one chaser, reserve-a-destination runs, 70% cruise and GRF-style support spacing would still be missing

### Takeaway
Relative to the described sim, FIFA's documented attacking off-ball layer adds: a space-ownership field (who reaches a point first) used for run targets, pass targets and lane-seeking support; carrier-facing-aware support/pass geometry; run intent controlled by tactics (urgency, support radius, formation freedom per third, risk, box-run timing/count); per-player phase offsets and work-rate frequency gates; attribute/trait-driven run preferences and passer perception; user-triggered runs and one-twos; and (as FIFA 13 later proved necessary) run commitment, curved/held onside runs and support for the *next* receiver.

### Cited Findings
- GRF's built-in teammate logic, which the reader's spacing is modelled on, is deliberately simple: "non-active players are also controlled by another rule-based bot ... such as running towards the ball when we are not in possession, or move forward together with our active player" — [Kurach et al., Google Research Football (AAAI 2020)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11180)
- Space ownership and run targets (EA patent): Voronoi/forecast-position ownership; run to the owned vertex beyond the offside line; collision-avoiding steering to nearest vertex; supporters move toward carrier-to-neighbour lanes; pass to the intersection of the carrier's facing vector with the receiver's cell; recompute staggered over frames — [Google Patents US7588493B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7588493B2/en)
- Arrival-time alternative with concrete constants (Namco, not EA): 0.5 s inertia projection, 15 m evaluation window, ability-speed division, 6 s "space" threshold on a pitch grid — [Google Patents US7390254B2](https://patents.google.com/patent/US7390254B2/en)
- Urgency states and position priority (FIFA 10): relaxed when not involved, alert as play nears; DMs hold cover until safe — [TheSixthAxis, EA Talk FIFA 10](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/2009/06/16/ea-talk-fifa-10/)
- Tactic knobs on support and runs (Speed/Passing/Positioning/CC Passing/Crossing/Shooting) — [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Custom Tactics](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-781-fifa-09-custom-tactics.html); FIFA 12 Run frequency / line sliders — [Neoseeker](https://www.neoseeker.com/forums/54562/t1676903-gameplay-slider-list/)
- Per-player levers (Work Rate frequency gate, attack/defence Positioning offsets that are "not runs") — [TheGameReviews FIFA 09 Editing Formations](https://www.thegamereviews.com/article-786-fifa-09-editing-formations.html)
- Trait/attribute-driven run preferences and Vision-gated passer perception (FIFA 12 PPI) — [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/87683/interview-with-fifa-12-producer-david-rutter/); [IGN](https://www.ign.com/wikis/fifa-12/Developer_Tips); [EA Backpage attributes](https://www.ea.com/news/fifa-12-attributes-guide-01)
- User-triggered run of the faced teammate; give-and-go with continued run — [FIFA 12 manual](https://manualmachine.com/electronicarts/eafifasoccer12/340817-user-manual); [FIFA 09 controls](https://fifa-soccer.ru/fifa09guide/FIFA_09_Basic_Controls.pdf)
- Run commitment, curved/held onside runs, run types, lane creation, two-plays-ahead support (FIFA 13 fixes to FIFA 12 behaviour) — [ESPN McHardy](https://www.espn.com/blog/playbook/tech/post/_/id/570/fifa-13-five-things-you-need-to-know); [UltimateFIFA (archived)](https://web.archive.org/web/20130826080611id_/http://ultimatefifa.com/2012/fifa-13-ai-improvements-attacking-intelligence/); checked runs and backline-watching (FIFA 14) — [EA FIFA 14 Teammate Intelligence](https://www.ea.com/news/fifa-14-teammate-intelligence)
- Teammate-yields-to-user target with teamwork-gated delay (Konami, not EA) — [Google Patents US20100240429A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20100240429A1/en)

### Inferences (concrete gaps in the described sim, in suggested priority order)
1. Ownership field: compute a cheap "who gets there first" map (Voronoi on velocity-offset positions, or Namco-style arrival time on a coarse grid) and derive run end-points (owned points beyond the offside line), pass targets (owned points along the carrier's facing/lead line) and support points (owned points near carrier→neighbour lanes); the sim's GRF spacing has no notion of contested space.
2. Run commitment with bounded re-evaluation: pick an end-point, keep it unless invalidated, and curve/hold rather than abandon when a defender blocks or offside threatens (FIFA 12's zigzag is the documented failure mode of a per-tick re-pick).
3. Support for the likely next receiver, not only the carrier, with urgency rising as the ball approaches (FIFA 10 urgency; FIFA 13 two-plays-ahead).
4. Tactic parameters as explicit weights: advance urgency (Speed), support radius (short → nearest 2–3 come to feet; long → far players spread, near players push for knock-downs), formation-slot freedom per third (Organised/Free Form), final-third risk (safe-support vs in-behind), box-run timing/count on crosses (near post, back post, centre), layoff/cutback spots (Shooting).
5. Per-player gates and offsets: attacking work rate as run-consideration frequency; defensive work rate as recovery priority; attack- and defence-phase positional offsets added to the slot.
6. Trait-keyed run targets and passer perception radius (PPI/Vision).
7. User-triggered run of the faced teammate (L1) and give-and-go continuation.
8. A yield rule for teammates standing inside the human's intended destination (Konami radius R / displacement L ≥ R), complementing the existing reserve-a-destination rule.

### Gaps
- No peer-reviewed paper describing FIFA's off-ball attacking AI was found in the academic search; results concerned GRF, physics-based controllers and tracking analytics, none naming FIFA's internals.
- No EA numbers exist for support radius, run lengths, or re-evaluation intervals; any constants adopted will have to come from the Namco patent (0.5 s, 15 m, 6 s) or be tuned against the reader's own metrics.
