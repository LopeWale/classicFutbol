Labelling convention used throughout: **[official]** = EA/Konami first-party text (Pitch Notes, manuals, patents, EA SEED papers); **[community]** = third-party transcriptions of in-game text, slider projects, fan wikis; **[estimate]** = my inference, not found in any source. ## Key Question 1 — Title-by-title chronology: what each FIFA/FC team-AI system does, what parameters exist, and what EA says about how positioning is computed

### Takeaway
EA's outfield positioning has been a hand-authored, formation-anchored system since at least FIFA 10 that has been incrementally layered with: attribute-scaled "personality" (FIFA 11, 21, 22), contain/jockey defending with a single AI "second man" (FIFA 12–13), multi-frame threat evaluation for marking (FIFA 14), explicit space-ranking and run-timing prediction (FIFA 17), a parameterised tactics layer with 1–10 then 1–100 depth/width sliders (FIFA 19 → FIFA 22), a "Tactical AI" rewrite that keeps the defensive block as a unit and increases attacker decision rate (FIFA 22), and finally a role-based system (FC 25 "FC IQ") in which 52 Role+Focus combinations drive off-ball behaviour via per-role "activity maps", Role Familiarity feeds 10–40% of every positioning formula, and team width is no longer a slider but an emergent property of the chosen Roles. EA SEED stated in 2025 that FC 25's outfield players still "have advanced, hand-crafted AI" (only the GK positioning is RL-trained in FC 26).

### Inferences
- The lineage is consistent: a formation "home" position per player (the out-of-possession anchor), per-phase offsets (in-possession shape), and attribute-scaled behaviour gates. FC 25's "activity map per Position×Role×Focus" is best read as a 2-D spatial prior (where the player is allowed/encouraged to be in each phase) layered on the formation slot — i.e. a role-specific heat-map/zone, not a free optimiser. [estimate]
- EA's own description of FIFA 22 ("up to 6 times more decisions per second") and FIFA 14 ("four times faster… multiple frames per second") indicates decision cadence is an explicit tunable, decoupled from the 60 Hz physics tick; a deterministic sim can likewise run team-decision passes at a lower fixed rate (e.g. every N ticks, staggered per player) without changing the physics step. [estimate]
- "Transition time" (FC 25 Build-Up Style: patient/normal/quick; Role Familiarity slows it) implies positioning targets are blended from defensive-shape to attacking-shape over a time constant that depends on team setting and per-player familiarity, rather than snapped. [estimate]

### Gaps
- EA has never published the actual positioning algorithm (utility functions, cost maps, or slider-to-metre mappings) for any title; Pitch Notes describe behaviours, not computation. The FIFA 17 "ranking attacking space" and FIFA 22 "Threat-Based Defending"/"Space-Based Attacking" are named but undocumented.
- The FIFA 17 developer interview on fifagamenews.com returned HTTP 500; only EA's own summary was retrievable.
- No official FC 24 "Tactical Vision" feature text was found.

## Key Question 2 — Who presses, who covers, how the line steps/drops, how "teammate contain" picks its player, how two defenders are kept off one attacker (FIFA/FC)

### Takeaway
Across FIFA 12→FC 26 the first presser is the user (or, for CPU, a single selected player); the "second man" (Teammate Contain, R1/RB) is always the nearest AI defender, and from FIFA 22 it is budgeted by a per-player Contain Stamina derived from Defensive Awareness, Defensive Work Rate and remaining stamina; FIFA 23 added a two-player "Partial Team Press" that marks passing options rather than the ball. The rest of the block holds formation shape, shifts toward the ball side by the width setting, and the line height is a team scalar (1–10 in FIFA 19, 1–100 from FIFA 22, four Defensive Approach bands with defaults 25/50/70/95 in FC 25) with explicit run-tracking rules per band. Duplicate-marking avoidance is described (FIFA 14 "reduce the chance of someone being left wide-open", FIFA 21 "Full back Cooperation", FC 25 "cover distance, smart angle marking") but never specified algorithmically.

### Cited Findings
- Second-man selection (FIFA 13): Teammate Contain asks "the closest AI-controlled defender" to contain; it "won't full-on press the ball, just attempt to slow the play and protect crucial areas". Author warns that near your own box the chosen player is often a CB who "already [has] a man to mark" — i.e. selection is proximity-based, not role-based [official EA blog] — [EA Backpage FIFA 13 Defending Tips](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/news/fifa-13-defending-tips-the-backpage)
- FIFA 22 contain parameters: Contain Stamina drains while held; on depletion "they will go back to their regular instructions and enter a cooldown period for a few seconds"; proximity, urgency, stamina pool and cooldown all scale with Defensive Awareness, Defensive Work Rates and in-match Stamina [official] — [FIFA 22 Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/ea-play/news/pitch-notes-fifa-22-gameplay-deep-dive)
- FIFA 23 Partial Team Press: two players mark "the opponent's passing options" until release / switch / Contain Stamina exhausted [official] — [FIFA 23 Deep Dive](https://www.ea.com/en-us/news/pitch-notes-fifa-23-gameplay-deep-dive)
- Block behaviour (FIFA 22): "maintain the shape of the formation when moving across the pitch, cover vacant spaces, mark each defending zone accordingly"; Operation Sports' footage reading: "As the ball is played towards the sidelines, the AC Milan midfielders move in unison with the wide players cutting off passing lanes" but midfielders did not drop once the line was broken [community observation] — [OS FIFA 22 pt 1](https://www.operationsports.com/fifa-22-gameplay-improvements-deep-dive-part-1)
- Zonal vs man: FIFA 19 Defensive Width (in-game text): narrow → "players will pinch in on the far side to cover teammates and allow for ball side pressure"; wide → "Players on the far side will mark opponents tightly, sacrificing cover" [community transcription] — [FIFPlay FIFA 19 Tactics](https://www.fifplay.com/fifa-19-tactics/). FIFA 20 Overload Ball Side: shape "shift[s] towards the ball, potentially ignoring defensive Player Instructions and marked opposition players" [official] — [D-Pad Tactics](https://ea.com/news/pitch-notes-d-pad-tactics)
- Press triggers (FIFA 19 defensive styles): heavy touch / loose ball / difficult pass facing own goal; after possession loss "approximately 7 seconds"; constant [community transcription] — [FIFPlay FIFA 19 Tactics](https://www.fifplay.com/fifa-19-tactics/). ### Inferences
- A faithful model of EA's scheme is a three-tier defensive assignment: (1) one "engager" on the ball (user, or CPU nearest-by-time-to-ball), (2) at most one or two "secondary pressers" (nearest AI defender(s) to the ball-carrier or to passing options), budgeted by a per-player stamina pool and cooldown so they rejoin shape, and (3) everybody else solving a zonal task: formation slot, translated toward the ball laterally by a width-dependent gain and longitudinally by line height, with man-marking only when an opponent enters the zone and the "wide" setting favours tight marking over cover. [estimate, consistent with FIFA 19/20/22/FC 25 texts]
- Line-height bands with explicit run-tracking rules (drop with runs / flexible / rarely / never + step-up) map naturally to a per-tick rule: `target_line_y = clamp(base(line_height), deepest_tracked_runner_y + margin, …)` where the margin and whether runners are tracked depend on the band. [estimate]
- "Cover distance, smart angle marking" (FC 25) and the FIFA 12 "stand to the attacker's stronger side" / "show infield" descriptions suggest marking target points computed as offsets along the attacker→goal line (goal-side) with lateral bias toward the attacker's strong foot or toward teammates — matching the explicit Konami patent geometry in Key Question 5. [estimate]
- To avoid two defenders chasing one attacker, EA's described signals are: role (Stopper vs Defender), a "most dangerous player" ranking evaluated over multiple frames, teammate-awareness ("Full back Cooperation"), and the stamina budget that forces secondary pressers back; an assignment pass (e.g. greedy by threat then by time-to-reach, with one marker per target) reproduces these qualitatively. [estimate]

### Gaps
- No official statement of how the CPU-controlled team chooses its first presser (likely nearest/time-to-ball, as Konami's patents and the FIFA 13 contain text suggest, but EA does not say).
- No published metre values for line height/depth/width in any FIFA/FC title; the 1–100 FC 25 Line Height and OS slider values are unitless.
- The FIFA 19 "approximately 7 seconds" press-after-loss duration is community-transcribed in-game text, not Pitch Notes.

## Key Question 3 — Off-ball runs and spacing in attack: triggers, how many run, how they avoid the same space, how width is kept (FIFA/FC Roles)

### Takeaway
EA's attacking off-ball model (FIFA 13→FC 26) is a run/support system where each AI teammate chooses between "come short" (support) and "get in behind/into box" (run) based on team setting (Build-Up Style / Chance Creation), role (FC 25 Roles with "stay wide", "cut inside", "underlap/overlap", "get in the box", "come short" tags), per-player Positioning attribute and Role Familiarity (which gate "types of attacking runs, selecting the best spot to make a run, best spot to position, better support range, timing of attacking runs, better notion of space"), plus prediction of the passer's readiness and a ranking of attacking space (FIFA 17). Width is emergent from Roles in FC 25 (no width slider), and FC 26 explicitly re-spaced role positions "to avoid congestion in the wings" and lets players override role zones when a better run exists.

### Cited Findings
- Run timing/prediction (FIFA 17 AIS): "Understanding when the dribbler is ready to make a pass so teammates time their run correctly" and "Understanding and ranking attacking space"; previously "AI players would not take the chance to run into an area if there was a defender already there. Now, how the defensive line sits will factor in how a player gets into space" [official] — [FIFA 17 AIS interview](https://www.ea.com/games/fifa/news/fifa-17-active-intelligence-system)
- Curved/checked runs along the line to beat offside (FIFA 13/14) [official] — [FIFA 13 innovations](https://www.ea.com/en-ca/news/ea-sports-unveils-fifa-13-innovations); [FIFA 14 PWNED](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/news/ea-sports-fifa-14-new-gameplay-additions-pwned)

### Inferences
- The EA model for "who runs" is per-player and role-gated rather than a global quota: each AI teammate evaluates run vs support on its own cadence, with role tags acting as hard/soft constraints ("stay wide", "no forward runs", "get in the box") and team Build-Up Style shifting the prior. FC 26's note that runs now "more consistently run into open space" and that players can "go beyond" the role zone implies a space-scoring pass (cf. FIFA 17 "ranking attacking space") that is consulted after the role prior. [estimate]
- Collision/overlap of runs is handled implicitly by (a) distinct activity maps per slot (left vs right Box-to-Box differ), (b) role lateral rules (Half-Winger moves to weak-foot side; Wide Half moves to side needing support), (c) support-range tuning by familiarity, and (d) FC 26's explicit position re-spacing — i.e. EA tunes zone separation by hand rather than solving a global assignment. A deterministic sim could instead reserve a target cell per runner (first-come or priority by role/familiarity) to guarantee uniqueness. [estimate]
- "Transition time" as a team scalar modulated per player means in-possession shape is approached with heterogeneous speeds, which is what produces staggered (realistic-looking) runs rather than a synchronous shape snap. [estimate]

### Gaps
- No EA source gives the number of simultaneous runners, run-duration, abort rules, or the metric used to rank space.
- The per-role "activity maps" are shown as images in the Pitch Notes; their numeric extents are not published.

## Key Question 4 — Konami PES / eFootball: Team Spirit, Fluid Formation, Advanced Instructions, Team Instructions, Authentic Team Styles, Team Playstyles and Playing Styles

### Takeaway
Konami's system is more explicitly parameterised than EA's in the PES 2016–2021 era: two possession phases with separate formations (Fluid Formation: kick-off / in possession / out of possession), categorical Team Instructions (Attacking Style, Build Up, Attacking Area, Positioning; Defensive Style, Containment Area, Pressuring) plus three 1–10 sliders (Support Range, Defensive Line, Compactness), up to 2 attacking + 2 defensive Advanced Instructions that change specific players' zones (False Winger, Hug the Touchline, Anchoring, Tiki-Taka, Deep Defensive Line, Swarm the Box, Gegenpress, etc.), 3 switchable Preset Tactics, and a Team Spirit (0–100) chemistry scalar. eFootball (2021→) collapsed this into 5–6 Team Playstyles (Possession Game, Quick Counter, Long Ball Counter, Out Wide, Long Ball, + Overload from v6.0.0) defined by behaviour in four phases, and per-player Playing Styles (Anchor Man, Box-to-Box, Hole Player, The Destroyer, Orchestrator, Prolific Winger, Roaming Flank, Fox in the Box, Target Man, Dummy Runner, etc.) that "influence how a player positions themselves, moves off the ball".

### Inferences
- Konami's PES-era design is directly translatable to parameters for a sim: two shape templates (in/out of possession) blended on possession change; three scalar sliders (Support Range ≈ attacker spread radius around ball carrier; Defensive Line ≈ line y; Compactness ≈ inter-player distance scale when defending); binary switches for pressing style, containment side-bias and first-defender aggressiveness; and per-player zone overrides (Advanced Instructions) that explicitly describe compensatory movement by neighbours (fullback pushes when winger comes inside; midfielder drops when fullbacks go; teammates fill space vacated by a False 9) — i.e. hand-authored "if slot X leaves its zone, slot Y covers" rules. [estimate]
- eFootball's Team Playstyles are state machines over four phases (defending / build-up / attack / transition after gain / transition after loss) with per-phase line height, press intensity and run-vs-support bias; this is a compact way to encode coherent team behaviour and avoid swarming (press triggers "conservative" vs "swarm"). [estimate]

### Gaps
- Konami publishes very little first-party text; all parameter semantics above are community transcriptions of in-game help text (reliable but unverified against the manual). I found no Konami "Pitch Notes"-style developer explanation of the positioning algorithm, nor any official description of "Authentic Team Styles" beyond the PES 2019 manual's note that some teams have pre-set, auto-triggered Advanced Instructions.
- No official eFootball page listing Playing Style behaviours was found; the Konami overview page only names the Team Playstyle feature.
- Team Spirit's effect on positioning (beyond "how well squad members play together") is not specified anywhere I could find.

## Key Question 5 — Patents: EA and Konami (and Sega) on team positioning, marking, pressing and formation deployment

### Takeaway
The most informative public specification of a football-game positioning engine is EA's own 2001 patent US 6,558,258 / US 2003/0083117 (inventors Jason Rupert and Geoff Harrower), which claims a tactical engine built on six Voronoi diagrams (each team with/without goalkeepers, and both teams combined) recomputed every frame using momentum-offset sites, used for positioning, offensive runs, passing lanes, marking ("who owns a point… who can get to that point first"), pattern matching and collision avoidance. Konami's 2008–2011 patents (Tadakatsu Izumi, Shin Tsuchiya) specify concrete press/mark control: select the nearest defender; keep him at distance ≥R on the ball→goal line for a reference period before closing; inhibit pressing inside a goal-proximity restriction region whose radius grows with the score lead (D2 < D4 < D5) and expires after ~10 s; place a marker on the target→goal line (goal-side mode) or the target→ball line (intercept mode) at a fixed offset; and generate pass-receiving position candidates around the ball-carrier filtered by opponent proximity. Sega's US 7,300,345 covers auto-assigning players to formation slots by ability values. EA's later patents (2012–2025) cover imitation-learning/adaptive NPC control generally, not football positioning specifically.

### Cited Findings
**EA — Voronoi tactical engine**

Abstract: "spatial analysis and/or tactical analysis to determine a move of a computer-controlled entity"; "the game space might be a two-dimensional soccer field, wherein the game elements are players having associated teams" [official patent] — [Google Patents US20030083117A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/US20030083117A1/en)

### Inferences
- The Rupert/Harrower patent is the only first-party document that names a spatial representation (Voronoi ownership / dominant region) for FIFA-era positioning. Its 2001 date (FIFA 2002/2003 era) plus Rupert's continued appearance on EA's 2018–2019 ML patents suggests the dominant-region idea has long been in EA's toolbox; whether current FC titles still compute Voronoi cells is not stated. [estimate]
- Konami's patents give directly implementable fixed-point-friendly rules: nearest-defender selection, contain radius R on the ball→goal line with a timed switch to closing, score-dependent restriction zones and hold timers, and marker placement at a fixed offset along T→G or T→B. These are exactly the "who presses / where does the marker stand / when does he close" decisions the sim needs, and they are stated at 1/60 s update granularity. [estimate]

- No EA patent specifically claiming FC IQ's Role/Familiarity system or any "team width from roles" method was found (searched Google Patents/USPTO via Parallel; results were general NPC/ML patents).

## Key Question 6 — EA SEED research and academic pitch-control models relevant to team positioning

### Takeaway
EA SEED's published football work is (a) a sample-efficient offline-bootstrapped SAC method that trains the FC 25/26 goalkeeper positioning policy (10% better save rate vs built-in AI, 50% faster training, >300 unit-test scenarios, reward = sparse save + dense centre-line coverage + smoothness penalties; policy outputs a target position plus movement intensity, 5-layer 256-unit MLPs, 60 Hz with action repeat 5), and (b) a 2025 MARL study on Google Research Football improving TiZero's exploration (RND +18.8% sample efficiency), whose reward explicitly includes a "Grouping Penalty" against teammates clustering. Neither replaces FC's hand-crafted outfield AI. Academic pitch-control (Spearman 2018; Fernández & Bornn 2018) formalises "who owns space" as a probabilistic/continuous field — the same quantity the 2001 EA Voronoi patent approximates discretely.

### Cited Findings
- Goalkeeper RL (SEED + EA Sports, arXiv 2510.23216, Oct 2025, Sestini, Bergdahl, Barrette-LaPierre, Fuchs, Chen, Jones, Gisslén): "We evaluate our method training a goalkeeper agent in EA SPORTS FC 25… Our agent outperforms the game's built-in AI by 10% in ball saving rate… trains agents 50% faster… the method has been adopted for use in the most recent release of the series." "While the outfield players in the game have advanced, hand-crafted AI, the goalkeeper system suffers from suboptimal behavior." Training used "a low-resolution version of the game… unlocked frame rates, speeding up the simulation by a factor of three." Offline bootstrap: "collect a dataset of N transitions running the built-in AI for each curriculum phase". Reward: "a sparse component for saving goals, a dense component for encouraging play near the middle line, and penalty components for reducing noisy movements… human players need to conserve energy, whereas the agent has infinite stamina". Output: "The goalkeeper positioning system outputs a target position… and the desired intensity of the movement." Results table: Quantitative success 73.46% vs 65.58%; vs human (400 games) goal-conceding 25.25% vs 29.10%, saving 54.12% vs 48.27%; expert test suite 91.5% vs 94.0% ("The built-in AI and the tests… are deterministic"). Hyper-parameters: 5-layer 256-wide MLPs with layer norm; action repetitions 5; max timesteps 300; γ 0.997; LR 5e-4; curriculum phases 3 with [11,18,25] scenarios [official research paper] — [arXiv 2510.23216v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23216v3)
- EA SEED news (2026-03-26): "trained them in hundreds of thousands of in-game situations to find the best positioning, including those little micro steps world-class keepers use to close the angle" (Mike Jones); "a 10% improvement in ball saving rate, trains 50% faster… can be trained overnight. A robust validation system featuring over 300 'unit-test' scenarios" [official] — [EA SEED, FC 26 goalkeepers](https://www.ea.com/seed/news/fc-26-goalkeepers)
- MARL football (SEED, arXiv 2503.13077, Mar 2025, Baghi, Sjölund, Bergdahl, Gisslén, Sestini): "TiZero takes 40 days to train high-quality policies for a football environment"; RND "improves training sample efficiency by 18.8%"; "it has proven difficult to create human-like, emergent and representative team-based behavior for football via hand-crafted and scripted methods". Base reward: ±1 goals, "Hold-Ball Reward (+0.0001 per time-step…), Passing-Ball Reward (+0.05…), Grouping Penalty (−0.001 when teammates cluster too closely, with a threshold of 0.05 distance units), Out-of-Bounds Penalty (−0.001)"; observations 330 floats (actor) / 220 (critic) incl. offside flags; GRF heuristic AI "actively controls the selected player while applying basic football behavior to non-active ones" with a difficulty factor controlling "faster decision-making and reaction times" [official research paper] — [arXiv 2503.13077v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13077v1)
- Pitch control literature: Spearman (2018) "Beyond Expected Goals" (MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference) introduced a physics-based pitch-control model yielding on- and off-ball metrics, using 58 matches of 2017–18 tracking data; Fernández & Bornn (2018) "Wide Open Spaces" (Sloan) treats control "as a continuous quantity rather than a binary one", "based on each player's influence on a point of the pitch at a given time", and valued space occupied by players like Messi and Iniesta [secondary sources] — [SMGT432 lecture slides](https://saberpowers.github.io/teaching/smgt432/lecture/tracking/slides.pdf); [Frontiers in Sports and Active Living 2021](https://www.frontiersin.org/articles/10.3389/fspor.2021.676179); [UvA MSc thesis (Dop)](https://staff.science.uva.nl/a.visser/education/masterProjects/Thesis_Ramon_Dop_MsDS.pdf)
- Open implementation: "A Python implementation of… Wide Open Spaces… Currently only contains the team pitch control function (equation 2 in the paper)… optimising the code for both Wide Open Spaces & Beyond Expected Goals (Spearman, Sloan, 2018)… a full game done in about 30 seconds" on GPU [community code] — [GitHub anenglishgoat/Metrica-pitch-control](https://github.com/anenglishgoat/Metrica-pitch-control)

### Inferences
- The SEED GK work is a template for how EA integrates learned components: a positioning policy that emits a target point + urgency (not raw locomotion), bootstrapped from the existing scripted AI, validated by a deterministic scenario suite. The same interface (target position + intensity per player per decision tick) is what a deterministic sim should expose for outfield positioning so that scripted and learned policies are interchangeable. [estimate]
- The MARL "Grouping Penalty" and the GK "smoothness penalties" are explicit codifications of the anti-bunching and anti-jitter objectives; a scripted equivalent is a per-player repulsion/minimum-separation term plus hysteresis on target changes. [estimate]
- Voronoi ownership (EA 2001) is the deterministic, integer-friendly degenerate case of pitch control (time-to-intercept with zero reaction/acceleration terms); a fixed-point sim can use momentum-offset Voronoi or a coarse time-to-reach grid in place of Spearman's probabilistic sigmoid. [estimate]

### Gaps
- Original Spearman 2018 and Fernández & Bornn 2018 PDFs were not retrievable here; formulas (Spearman's time-to-intercept sigmoid parameters, F&B's Gaussian influence area) are therefore not quoted. Secondary sources agree on the qualitative model only.
- No EA SEED publication on outfield team positioning (imitation or RL) for FC was found; EA has publicly referenced xG in match stats (FIFA 22/23) but I found no EA statement that it uses pitch-control/EPV models for in-game positioning.

- Slider→metre mapping [estimate]: none is official. - Press budget [estimate]: FIFA 22's Contain Stamina + "a few seconds" cooldown and FIFA 19's ~7 s post-loss press window suggest modelling secondary pressing as a per-player budget of a few seconds with a several-second cooldown, scaled by Defensive Awareness/Work Rate.

### Gaps

- Operation Sports values are tuned for "feel" on specific builds and change per title update; they indicate direction of effect, not calibration.
